COMMON QUESTIONSTHE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Clear answers.
Before you decide.

Can I change my rates later?

Yes. The included Pricing Studio lets you edit room fees, extras, cleaning type, recurring frequency, property condition, pet surcharges and minimum charges. You can export a revised settings file and update your hosted widget.

Does it replace our existing website?

No. The standard offer is a calculator page you can link from your website or embed where your current website platform supports it. We check compatibility before payment.

Where do quote requests go?

The basic setup can open a prepared email for the customer to send to your business inbox. An optional Google Sheets workflow can collect requests automatically, subject to Google account approval and daily quotas. We confirm the chosen method during setup.

Does the displayed estimate guarantee the price?

No. Customers see a ballpark range using your pricing rules, and can review and print its breakdown. Your team confirms a final quote after reviewing the property and service requirements.

What are the ongoing costs?

There is no recurring QuoteNest platform charge for this package. Your existing hosting, website-builder features, domain, or optional third-party services can have separate fees. These are reviewed before setup.

Can customers pay and reserve a cleaning?

QuoteNest currently handles estimates and quote inquiries. The standard package does not collect card payments, schedule appointments, or integrate a CRM. Those features need separate scope.

QuoteNest provides indicative estimates and inquiry capture. It is not a calendar booking system or a payment processor for your cleaning customers. No increase in bookings is guaranteed. The US$99 figure is introductory service pricing; applicable taxes, if any, are confirmed before payment.
