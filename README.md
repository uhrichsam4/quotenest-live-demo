# QuoteNest — an instant cleaning quote widget

[Try the live calculator](https://uhrichsam4.github.io/quotenest-live-demo/embed.html) ·
[See the product website](https://uhrichsam4.github.io/quotenest-live-demo/) ·
[One-time setup offer](https://uhrichsam4.github.io/quotenest-live-demo/setup.html)

QuoteNest helps cleaning companies show **indicative price ranges** before a
customer requests a final, owner-approved quote.

### What can customers estimate?

- Standard, deep-clean and move-in/move-out visits
- Whole-home versus selected-room cleaning
- Bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens and living areas
- Weekly or biweekly recurring cleans
- Pet surcharges, oven/fridge/windows add-ons
- Light, typical or heavy property condition
- A minimum job charge set by the business owner

The [Pricing Studio](https://uhrichsam4.github.io/quotenest-live-demo/studio.html)
lets a business change the logo text, pricing rules, email inbox and accent color
in the browser, then download its own `config.js`.

### What does the one-time setup include?

The introductory **US$99** offer covers tailoring the brand, owner-provided
rates, and basic linking or iframe installation where supported. The buyer and
provider agree on the exact scope before arranging payment.

There is no QuoteNest subscription and no payment is collected by this demo.
Existing hosting, website builders, or other service providers may have
separate charges.

### Important details

This repository is a public demonstration. Its default example company and
price rules are fictional. Demo mode does not submit customer inquiries;
the client must configure a real email destination or the optional Google
Apps Script lead workflow. The estimator is not a payment or reservation tool,
and increased bookings are not guaranteed.
